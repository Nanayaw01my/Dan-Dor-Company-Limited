About Us

Ghana's Premier Solar Energy Company

Dan & Dor Solar Company Limited is a proudly Ghanaian solar energy company headquartered in Bogoso, Western Region. We were founded with a clear mission: to make clean, reliable, and affordable solar energy accessible to every Ghanaian — from the individual homeowner to large-scale commercial enterprises.

We understand the energy challenges facing Ghanaian families and businesses. Rising electricity tariffs and frequent power outages are disruptions we believe solar energy can solve. Our team of certified engineers and technicians delivers world-class installations backed by outstanding after-sales support.

Reliability

Systems built to last, backed by comprehensive warranties and ongoing maintenance support.

Affordability

Flexible payment plans and credit options to make solar accessible to everyone.

Quality

We use only industry-leading, certified solar products from top global manufacturers.

Benefits

Why Go Solar?

Solar energy is more than a trend — it's a smart, long-term investment for your family and business.

Save on Electricity Bills

Dramatically reduce or eliminate your monthly electricity bills. Most customers see savings within the first month of installation.

Clean, Renewable Energy

Solar power produces zero emissions. By going solar, you're actively contributing to a cleaner, greener Ghana for future generations.

Power During Outages

Never suffer through another power outage. With battery storage, your home or business stays fully powered even when the grid goes down.

Long-Term Investment

Solar panels increase property value and pay for themselves over time. With a 25+ year lifespan, the returns are exceptional.

Ghana's Perfect Climate

Ghana enjoys abundant sunshine year-round, making it one of the best locations in the world for solar energy generation.

Low Maintenance

Solar systems require minimal maintenance. With no moving parts and durable panels, your system will run efficiently for decades.
